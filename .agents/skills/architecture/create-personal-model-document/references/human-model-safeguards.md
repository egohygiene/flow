# Human-Model Safeguards

## Core Distinction

    person
        ≠
    system representation
        ≠
    profile
        ≠
    persona
        ≠
    prediction

## Safeguards

- preserve agency and meaningful choice
- distinguish provided, observed, and inferred information
- preserve confidence and uncertainty
- allow correction and contestability
- expect identity and context to change
- avoid unsupported sensitive inference
- avoid diagnosing or essentializing people
- avoid treating engagement as benefit
- avoid one universal model of well-being

## Additional Review Triggers

- medical or mental-health claims
- disability
- minors
- legal eligibility
- employment or financial outcomes
- identity classification
- biometric or highly sensitive data
