# Flow Manifesto

## Opening declaration

Powerful transformations should not require blind trust.

## What we believe

We believe a workflow can be ambitious and still be inspectable. Specialized
tools can cooperate without surrendering their boundaries. Automation can
increase agency when it exposes its plan, preserves evidence, and leaves a path
back from failure.

We believe provenance is context, not clutter. An output should tell the truth
about the source it came from and the work that changed it.

## What we commit to

We commit to preserving originals, planning before execution, validating real
outcomes, designing stable machine contracts, and keeping every holon useful on
its own. We commit to explicit uncertainty and to learning from observed runs.

## What we refuse to normalize

We refuse silent destructive behavior, opaque shell pipelines, unbounded
arbitrary-command execution, false claims of provenance, hidden uploads,
unverifiable completion, and architectural coupling disguised as convenience.

## Invitation

Build tools that are strong alone and generous in composition. Make the system
easy to question, easy to inspect, and honest about what it knows.

## Closing statement

Define the flow. Preserve the evidence. Earn the output.

## Provenance and alignment

This manifesto derives from `flow-purpose`, `flow-vision`, `flow-principles`,
and `flow-pillars`. It expresses culture; enforceable rules remain owned by the
principles, foundations, decisions, and system contracts.
