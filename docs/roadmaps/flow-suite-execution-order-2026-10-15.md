# Flow suite provider execution order — October 15, 2026

This is the latest bounded execution-order refresh for the three independent
providers. It supplements the [October 6 full dependency map](flow-suite-dependency-map.md)
without replacing its preserved graph, node inventory or historical archive.
Live provider issues, branches and releases govern execution. This document
plans work; it does not resume every product issue or establish passing checks.

The finish lines remain separate: a useful independently installed provider,
the first installed Flow suite, the stable audited suite, and the full retained
product backlog. Optional features require an explicit keep, defer or decline
decision; they do not silently become baseline release prerequisites.

## Qualification and publication boundaries

When the owning issue resumes qualification, retain exact candidate/tool
versions, commands, counts, failures, skips and raw evidence. Distinguish
source review, synthetic execution, native host/model/volume qualification,
downloaded-release installation and actual downstream consumption.

- Run supported local compiler/MSRV, tests, lint, contracts, docs/package and
  applicable bounded corpus gates on the integrated candidate. Fix findings
  without lowering requirements, assertions or drift checks.
- Native APFS/physical-volume, Apple silicon, real provider/model and actual
  media behavior require their recorded native evidence. Emulation or synthetic
  faults cannot establish those claims.
- Required hosted/release gates remain required. Local equivalents do not
  silently replace declared hosted checks, signing/attestation, immutable-release
  settings or clean-install verification. Billing/hosted recovery is separate.
- [Renderflow's release guide](https://github.com/egohygiene/renderflow/blob/0427749a2cccececae2d69aa9fa0a02779603509/docs/release-candidate.md)
  requires exact-commit CI/docs/manual conformance, current scheduled evidence
  and verified downloaded artifacts on its declared Ubuntu target. Its current
  Cargo minimum is Rust 1.94; older contributor setup/release prose needs
  reconciliation under #419.
- Never reuse an old tag, receipt, provider version or read-only qualification
  to claim a new mutation/media capability. Publication and consequential
  effects retain their explicit owner authority.
